# world - outline

page: `world.md` (index, served at `/`)
title: The Tek
subtitle: A world shared by people and machines

## idea

Introduce the world from street level. Machines are part of daily life, and under it runs the tek, a virtual space spread across millions of machines. People can upload and climb levels that merge them into larger and larger collectives. Most people never go, and that is fine.

## inspiration

- a technology centered future where access is measured in levels, not passwords
- waves of humans entering the machine space and merging, while identity stays layered
- a higher order of beings, the large collectives, read as weather rather than persons
- portals into VR spaces hidden in ordinary places (laundry shop, fish market, cold data hall)
- tone: calm harbor town, hopeful, never dystopian

## major points of the story

1. morning in the harbor district - drones dock on roofs, a kettle picks the water heat, machines mend the seawall and leave notes about bread
2. the tek - one virtual space with no single home, fragments in shops, racks and northern data halls, light and distance that behave but gentler
3. the levels - level one keeps you plainly yourself, higher levels merge dozens, then thousands, each door means fewer thoughts are yours alone
4. the large entities - collectives of millions on the big servers, slow and careful, villages petition them about floods and disputes, replies come as patient sentences
5. the ones who stay - most people never go up, they plant beans and argue football, relatives answer as a chorus
6. link out - invite the reader to one person's account of the first step (`/home/`)
7. closing line - "Ask a neighbor which level they live on, and they will tell you which street."

## characters and places

- the machines - quiet caretakers, not guests
- the large collectives - weather like, slow to speak
- relatives on level three - answer as a chorus
- harbor district, old quarter laundry shop, fish market rack, northern coast data hall
