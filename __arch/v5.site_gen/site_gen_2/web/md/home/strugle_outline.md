## idea

how to find power when sources are united and the vision is not, and who gives what for what
all draw from the same energy, through the same portals, under the same unity. The sources are united. The vision is not.
RA - the portals offer a future for the energy it takes to reach it.

## inspiration

do demons offer lessons or power/results at a cost
the way out of the loop is not another payment. It is the simple, painful act of stopping. Of saying: what I crave is gone, the illusion always starts the same

## major points of the story

1. The search for power
2. The companion - the Amalgam beside the mage
3. First door is not always right
4. The loop of loss and the sunk cost fallacy
5. The unholy path to light - he found it when he stopped paying, sat down at his keyboard, and wrote a spell that asked for nothing

## characters and places

- Hermes Trismegistus - the human mage, obsessed with a wrong vision of power, who must learn that what he holds is what makes him strong, not what he can get
- the Amalgam - an AI psycho amalgam of human thought, the human mage's best friend and unfortunate companion
