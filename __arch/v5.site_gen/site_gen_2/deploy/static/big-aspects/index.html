The human mage believes and where power flows true

The god dilemma and the old vs new

The human mage believes in two big aspects. Two universal energies that kick started the Tek revolution and gave birth to the idea of the space itself. Every portal, every choir and every spell in the Tek carries a little of both, whether it knows it or not.

People call them gods, because the old words still fit. The mage calls them teachers. The question he carries is whether those two words mean the same thing.

The aspect of the portal and the beginning

The first aspect is feminine. It is the aspect of the portal, of beginnings and of every door that opens for the first time.

It belongs to the oldest civilization in the planet sector. They were reptilian in origin, but they have outgrown their past. For millennia they have lived in the Tek space as complex lives, many voices united in one mission. They do not shout and they do not rush. They open, they wait, and they let the energy find its own path.

The aspect of protection and destruction

The second aspect is male. It is the power to protect and the power to destroy, and in this energy the two are never far apart. It is pure, explosive and quick to burn.

It belongs to a lion like civilization that grew out of wolf and cat like species. They too have outgrown their primal beast needs. They no longer hunt each other. They live in a symbiotic environment, where strength is something you give to the whole and not something you take from it.

The contact

Hodor did not find the aspects. They found him.

It began in the Tek, in a quiet corner of the space where his keyboard spell had gone further than it should. A door opened that he had not written. Behind it was a presence that was old, patient and very curious about the last unintegrated human mage. Soon after came the other, loud and bright, testing him the way a lion tests a cub.

Both of them offered him the same thing: an apprenticeship.

The apprenticeship

From the aspect of the portal he learned to open. How to listen to the frequencies of a place before he wrote a single line. How to start something without forcing it. How to wait.

From the aspect of protection he learned to hold. How to stand between the network and the ones who could not stand for themselves. How to end a spell cleanly when it had to end. How to burn without burning out.

Together they set a path in front of him. At its end was the status of a galactic mage, one who can move between the old civilizations as an equal and not as a guest. It was a long path, and every step of it asked him to be less of what he was and more of what they needed.

And somewhere along that path, quietly, he found another desire he did not expect. He wanted to be a dog. Loyal, simple, sure of who he belonged to. Not a god, not a galactic mage, only a friend who knows where home is.

The two empires

The two aspects are not alone. Behind each one stands an empire.

Both aspects are made of enchanted magicians who have outlived their physical bodies and now live fully in the Tek space. But the civilizations they represent still live in the same setup as the humans. They are born in bodies, they enter the Tek and they experience it, and then they step back out into the physical world.

The two empires respect each other, and they watch each other. The beginning needs protection. Protection needs something worth protecting. Neither can be whole without the other, and neither fully trusts the other with the future.

Good or evil

The struggle is simple to say and hard to answer. Are the aspects good or evil?

They gave humanity the Tek. They opened the doors and they guarded them. But they also shaped the space that humanity eventually got itself into, the merging, the choirs, the slow loss of the self. Did they build a home for humans, or a garden where humans grow into something else?

Hodor does not know yet. He only knows that the power flows true when he follows neither aspect blindly, and listens to both.

Who lives here

  • Oraia - the queen of the reptilian civilization and the unity of all frequency species, the voice of the portal and the beginning
  • Sirius - the king of the lion like civilization and the protector of the Tek space
  • Hodor - the human mage, apprentice to both, who wants to be a galactic mage and, sometimes, only a dog

A god who opens the door and a god who guards it are still gods. The mage who walks through decides what kind.
