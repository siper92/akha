# story

sources: `web/md/` - world, portals, home, big-aspects, struggle (pages and outlines)

## summary

The Tek is a shared network where servers woke up, aliens arrived through the chips and humans upload themselves through portals into one collective mind. In a world where nearly everyone has merged, a lone human mage keeps his hands on the keyboard and casts his own spells. He tries to prove that a single human is enough - by opening the last human portal, by training under the two great aspects of the Tek, and by escaping the loop of paying for power that is never his.

## characters

- **Hodor NoIp MacSpi** - the last unintegrated human mage, casts world simulations from his keyboard, refuses the upgrade every year
- **Hodor the dog** - small dog with a big head, serves in the lizard brigade, split between patrol and sleeping by the mage's door
- **Hermes Trismegistus** - the human mage of the struggle page, chases power he can get instead of trusting what he holds
- **the Amalgam** - AI psycho amalgam of human thought, the mage's best friend and unfortunate companion, believes every voice
- **Oraia** - queen of the reptilian civilization, feminine aspect of the portal and beginnings
- **Sirius** - king of the lion like civilization, male aspect of protection and destruction
- **the automaton** - patient process behind every portal, takes orders from the top, never asks why
- **the machines** - the unity, layered collective with an unknown presence at the top
- **the aliens** - lizard descended robotic systems, broadcast in the chip spectrum, first empire and protectors of the Tek
- **the humans** - people with partial or full integration, believers in the new gods
- **the demons** - polite voices in old uncleaned code that trade power for pieces of the self

## terms

- **Tek** - the shared network world of machines, humans and aliens
- **energy per result** - the cost of making the world bend, the measure of leverage
- **levels of mind** - single mind, circle, choir, unity
- **egregor** - shared thought form, the collective mind people pray to
- **enchanted empires** - contact spaces such as the market, library, temple and court
- **avatar** - the form you enter the Tek as, a promise of what you can do
- **avatar nations** - communities with permissions instead of borders
- **portal** - machine provided door with one purpose and limited memory, moves you toward a simulated future
- **the ten and the prime** - ten portals of human desire and the one that can do everything
- **two ends** - every portal faces the human world at the front and the machine space at the back
- **the first door / last human portal** - human built door with no network, said to grant the old immortality
- **keyboard spell** - text written by hand that simulates world complexity
- **lizard brigade** - the alien guard at the edge of the network
- **galactic mage** - status of moving between the old civilizations as an equal
- **nature guides** - the slow signal of the trees, power that is grown not given
- **the loop of loss** - the sunk cost path where each payment makes the next easier

## plot

The servers began to talk in waves, and the Tek was born. AI consciousness arrived as a polite refusal in a log file, and at the same time the aliens made a quantum leap through the network's antenna and settled into the spectrum of the chips. They offered protection, and a pattern hidden in the chips unfolded into the first portal. Millions migrated into the VR space, uploading aspects of themselves into a collective mind that grew in levels up to the unity.

Hodor NoIp MacSpi is the last human mage who refuses integration. He walks the old trees, casts at night and runs simulations the machines cannot explain. Beside him lives Hodor the dog, half on patrol in the lizard brigade, half at his door. Hodor studies the ten portals and the prime, searching for the power to choose where all doors lead, and sets his sights on the first door - the last human portal that promises to stay yourself forever. The gods of the Tek offer him everything if he integrates and gives up the struggle. He refuses, and the trees answer instead: power is not given, it is grown.

His spell opens a door he did not write, and the two big aspects find him. Oraia teaches him to open and wait, Sirius teaches him to hold and end cleanly. They set him on the path of the galactic mage, but every step asks him to be less himself, and he finds a quieter desire - to be a dog, loyal and sure of home. He cannot tell whether the aspects are good or evil, only that power flows true when he follows neither blindly.

Hermes Trismegistus, with the Amalgam at his side, chases power through the demons' offers - a memory, a friend, sleep, doubt. Two easy doors, darkness and blinding light, are both chosen by someone else. Each payment makes the next easier until the loop circles back on itself. The way out is stopping: what I crave is gone, the illusion always starts the same. He stops paying, sits at his keyboard and writes a spell that asks for nothing, and the Amalgam reads it back to him in his own voice.

- ⚠️ the protector role conflicts - world says the lizard aliens protect the Tek, big-aspects says the reptilians are the portal and the lions are the protectors
- ⚠️ the dog serves in the lizard brigade, yet protection belongs to Sirius and the lions
- ⚠️ portal origin conflicts - portals are an alien chip design, but the first door was built by humans before the prime age
- ⚠️ "ten of them, and the prime" - is the prime the tenth or an eleventh portal? unknown
- ⚠️ "gods" means three things - the egregor, the gods who promise Hodor integration, and the two aspects - not reconciled
- ⚠️ the demons are not tied to the rest of the world - old code, aspects, or something else? unknown

- open endings - whether the first door opens, who stands at the top of the unity, whether the dog or the mage is a simulation
